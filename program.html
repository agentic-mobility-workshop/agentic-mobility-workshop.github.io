Program

A one-day, in-person workshop at AAAI-27 in Montréal on February 22 or 23, 2027 (exact day to be confirmed), running from 08:45 to 17:30.

Schedule

10:20-12:00 Poster Session I, coffee, and Contributed Spotlights I

14:10-15:30 Contributed Spotlights II and Poster Session II

17:00-17:30 Open roadmap discussion, awards, and closing

The final schedule with talk titles will be posted by January 15, 2027.

Format

Invited talks

Four invited talks with Q&A from leading researchers in AI, transportation, and human-AI interaction. Confirmed speakers are listed on the Speakers page, and additional speakers will be announced.

Meet the speakers

Posters and spotlights

Two poster sessions and two contributed spotlight blocks. Most accepted papers are presented as posters, and a smaller number are selected as spotlight talks. The poster sessions are mentoring-oriented, giving students and junior researchers direct feedback from senior participants.

Panel and roadmap

A moderated panel on which decisions agents should own and who is accountable, followed by an open roadmap discussion, awards, and closing remarks.

Venue

The workshop takes place at the AAAI-27 conference venue in Montréal, Canada. The workshop is in person only, and no virtual presentations will be offered, in line with AAAI-27 policy.
